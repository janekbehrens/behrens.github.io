FAQ

Common questions

More answers in the documentation, or write to support@janekbehrens.de.

How can it measure DORA™ metrics without CI/CD data?
From the Jira data you already have: released versions or status changes for deployments, the first “In progress” to the last “Done” for lead time, bugs raised within 14 days of a release (without releases: issues moved back out of Done) for the change failure rate, and incident tickets from created to resolved for time to restore. These are approximations from work items, measured the same way every time. The ⓘ on every tile says how the number is calculated.
What counts as a deployment?
You choose in the setup wizard: Jira versions marked as released (with a release date), or issues that first reach statuses you pick. With at least three releases in the last 90 days the wizard recommends releases, otherwise the status option. Both options are in Free.
Can it show whether our AI rollout changed delivery?
Yes, with Pro. Enter the rollout date in step 3 of the wizard. The AI Impact tab compares two equally long periods before and after it on all four metrics, with a rule-based verdict, and compares AI-involved issues with the rest. AI involvement comes from labels, issue types and app-account activity. Results are team totals, never per person.
How up to date are the numbers?
The first analysis reads up to 12 months of completed issues once. After that the data is refreshed every night for projects opened in the last 45 days, and the refresh button updates a project right away, at most once every 10 minutes. Jira reloads the gadgets every 3 hours.
Does the app change my issues or store personal data?
No. The app only reads Jira work data and never writes to your issues. It stores weekly aggregate numbers in Atlassian's Forge storage: no names and no account IDs. Details in the Trust Center.
Is it free?
Jira sites with up to 10 users pay nothing. Larger sites start with a free trial. Pro features are on while the site has an active subscription, the trial included. Without one, for example after the trial, the Overview gadget keeps working for one project with all four metrics, bands and trends.
