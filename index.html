FAQ

Common questions

Can I try an app before I install it?
Yes. Every app page has a live demo with sample data. It runs in your browser; nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere. When you want the app in your own Jira or Confluence, start the free trial on the Atlassian Marketplace.
Where does my data go?
The apps run on Atlassian Forge, so Atlassian hosts them. I don't operate servers of my own. Only CodeDoc AI talks to services outside Atlassian: the Git provider you connect and, if you choose, your own AI provider. The Trust Center lists the details per app.
What does it cost?
You install and pay through the Atlassian Marketplace; Atlassian handles billing and licenses. Every app starts with a free trial. Team Capacity, Visual Progress Tracker, DevOps Metrics and Velocity Chart are free for Jira sites with up to 10 users, and Epic Progress is free for Confluence sites with up to 10 users.
Do the apps work with Data Center or Server?
No. They are Forge apps for Jira Cloud and Confluence Cloud.
How do I get help?
Each app has documentation and a support page. Write to support@janekbehrens.de or book a free 30-minute call.
