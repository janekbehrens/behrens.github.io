Score right on the issue

Three fields, one per framework: Priority Scoring — RICE, Priority Scoring — WSJF and Priority Scoring — ICE. Move the sliders and the score updates while you drag; it is saved with the check mark.

The Rovo panel on the right is recreated for this image.

  • RICE: Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort
  • WSJF: (Business Value + Time Criticality + Risk Reduction) ÷ Job Size
  • ICE: Impact × Confidence × Ease

Board Health and a ranked backlog

The app page shows Board Health from 0 to 100 for a project and ranks the open issues you can see in Jira by the framework you pick.

  • Choose which of eight health criteria count, per project, or start from the Scrum or Kanban preset.
  • Filter the ranking by assignee or sprint; overdue issues are marked.
  • Export CSV gives you the ranking as text to copy into a spreadsheet.

Rovo suggests, explains, and applies if you want

Ask the Priority Scoring agent in Rovo for a score, an explanation, Board Health or the issues that need attention. A Jira admin decides what it may write.

The Rovo chat panel is recreated for this image.

  • Suggest only: Rovo suggests, you set the sliders.
  • Confirm: Rovo asks in the chat before it writes a score (default).
  • Auto: no question in the chat; Rovo applies scores after Atlassian's own confirmation, several issues per request up to your bulk limit (1 to 50).

Weights, thresholds and an audit trail

Jira admins tune the scoring for the whole site. Everyone else sees the settings read-only.

  • Weight every dimension except Effort and Job Size, from ×1 to ×5. Scores change on their next save.
  • Set where the score color turns from red to yellow to green.
  • Optional audit trail per issue, off by default.

FAQ

Common questions

More answers in the documentation, or write to support@janekbehrens.de.

Do I need Rovo?
Only for the AI features. The Priority Scoring agent needs Atlassian Rovo on your site. Scoring, the ranking, Board Health, the portfolio view and the gadgets work without it.
Can I change how scores are calculated?
The app uses the standard RICE, WSJF and ICE formulas. Jira admins can weight every dimension except Effort and Job Size (×1 to ×5) and set the color thresholds per framework.
Which Jira version do I need?
Priority Scoring needs Jira Cloud. It is a Forge app and runs on Atlassian's infrastructure. There is no Data Center or Server version.
Does any Jira data leave Atlassian's infrastructure?
No. The app runs on Atlassian Forge. When you use the Rovo agent, Atlassian's Rovo platform processes the issue data, not an external server. Details in the Trust Center.
