A heatmap for the sprint

One bar per person, highest share of capacity first: red is overloaded, amber near capacity, green on track, blue has room.

  • The header shows the team total: people, load against capacity, how many are overloaded.
  • Unassigned work gets its own grey row and is not part of the team total.
  • Several teams: add the gadget once per board, each with its own sprint and capacity.

Capacity per person PRO

One default capacity for the team, then individual numbers for part-timers, new joiners or anyone with less time.

  • Blank means the default. A person's number applies to every sprint until you change it.
  • The app does not read leave or holidays; for time off, enter the lower number yourself.
  • Your own thresholds: Over > 100%, Near ≥ 85% and Free < 50% by default.
More in the docs →

Uses your board's estimates

Workload comes from the estimation your board already has. Nothing to re-enter, nothing to sync.

  • Story points: company-managed Story Points and team-managed Story point estimate.
  • Time: boards that estimate with Original estimate are counted in hours.
  • Issue count for teams that don't estimate. Capacity uses the same unit.
More in the docs →

Remaining work mid-sprint PRO

Count only unfinished work leaves Done issues out, so each bar shows the work still open.

  • Applies to every bar and to the team total in the header.
  • An issue counts as done when its status is in Jira's Done category.
More in the docs →

Estimate vs. actual as CSV PRO

Export CSV downloads one row per person for the sprint on screen. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

  • Load, capacity, utilization %, status, issues, done and time spent in hours.
  • For the retro, switch to a closed sprint first: the menu lists the active sprint, upcoming ones and up to eight closed ones.

Setup

Running in three steps

1

Install

Get the app from the Atlassian Marketplace. A Jira site admin installs it once.

2

Add the gadget

On a dashboard you can edit, choose Add gadget and search for Team Capacity. No admin rights needed.

3

Pick a board

In Edit, choose a Scrum board, how to measure workload and a default capacity. Save, and the heatmap appears.

FAQ

Common questions

More answers in the documentation, or write to support@janekbehrens.de.

Do we have to re-enter estimates?
No. The gadget uses the estimation your board already has: story points, or time estimates converted to hours. Teams that don't estimate can count issues instead.
Does it work with team-managed projects?
Yes. Company-managed and team-managed Scrum boards both work, including the team-managed Story point estimate field. Classic Kanban boards are not listed, because they have no sprints.
Does it know about vacation or public holidays?
No. The app does not read leave or holidays. With Pro you lower that person's capacity yourself; the number applies to every sprint until you change it.
Does the gadget update on its own?
No. The footer shows when the numbers were read, for example "Updated 09:30". Click Refresh, or the refresh button of the gadget or the dashboard, to read Jira again.
Is it free?
Jira sites with up to 10 users pay nothing. Larger sites start with a free trial; after the trial it is priced per user. Without an active license the heatmap keeps working; per-person capacity, remaining work, custom thresholds and CSV export need Pro.
Does any data leave Atlassian?
No. The app is read-only and runs on Atlassian Forge. It checks each viewer's Jira project permissions on every request, so people only see boards whose project they can browse.
