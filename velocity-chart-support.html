Before you reach out

Most questions are answered in the documentation. Common ones:

  • "No closed sprints on this board yet" — velocity needs a Scrum board with at least one completed sprint. Kanban boards have no sprints.
  • Numbers look empty — your issues are probably estimated in another field than the board's. In the gadget's Edit form, set Estimation to the field your team actually uses (Custom numeric field, for example Story point estimate) or to Issue count. Estimation is a Pro setting.
  • A board is missing from the picker — only Scrum boards (company- and team-managed) in projects you can browse appear; classic Kanban boards are excluded because they have no sprints.
  • Team-managed boards — fully supported; they appear in the picker tagged "team-managed".

What to include in a bug report

  • Whether the board is company-managed or team-managed
  • Your estimation mode (Story Points / issue count / custom field)
  • What you expected vs. what you saw (a screenshot helps a lot)
